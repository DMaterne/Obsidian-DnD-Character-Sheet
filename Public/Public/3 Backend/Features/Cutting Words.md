---
name: Cutting Words
type: feature
source: Bard — College of Lore (SRD 5.1 / 2014)
level: 3
enabled: true
notes: When a creature you can see within 60 feet makes an attack roll, ability check, or damage roll,
  use your reaction and expend one Bardic Inspiration die to subtract the result. The creature must be
  able to hear you and not be immune to being charmed. Declare after the roll but before its result is
  announced.
