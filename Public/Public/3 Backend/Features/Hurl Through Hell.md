---
name: Hurl Through Hell
type: feature
source: Warlock — The Fiend (SRD 5.1 / 2014)
level: 14
enabled: true
notes: When you hit a creature with an attack, you can transport it through the lower planes. It vanishes
  until the end of your next turn, then returns to its prior space (or nearest available). If it is not
  a fiend, it takes 10d10 psychic damage from the experience. Once per long rest.
