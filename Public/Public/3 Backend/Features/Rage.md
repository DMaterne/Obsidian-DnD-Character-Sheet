name: "Rage"
type: "feature"
source: "Barbarian (PHB 2014)"
level: 1
enabled: true
notes: "As a bonus action, enter a rage for up to 1 minute while not wearing heavy armor. You gain advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws; bonus damage on Strength-based melee weapon attacks (+2 at levels 1-8, +3 at levels 9-15, +4 at levels 16-20); and resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage. You cannot cast or concentrate on spells while raging. Rage ends early if you fall unconscious or your turn ends without having attacked a hostile creature since your last turn or taken damage since then; you may also end it as a bonus action. Uses per long rest: 2 (levels 1-2), 3 (3-5), 4 (6-11), 5 (12-16), 6 (17-19), unlimited (20)."
