# Soul of Artifice

Your bond with magic items protects you.

The feature interacts with your attuned magic items, improving your saving throws and providing a powerful defensive benefit when you would otherwise be reduced to 0 hit points.

Automatic handling can be added later once saving-throw effects and combat reactions are expanded.
