---
name: Nature's Sanctuary
type: feature
source: Druid — Circle of the Land (SRD 5.1 / 2014)
level: 14
enabled: true
notes: When a beast or plant creature attacks you, it must make a Wisdom saving throw against your druid
  spell save DC. If it fails, it must choose a different target or miss. Once it succeeds, it is immune
  to this effect for 24 hours. Creature is aware of this effect before choosing to attack.
