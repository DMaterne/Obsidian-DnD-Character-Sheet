---
name: "Intimidating Presence"
type: "feature"
source: "Barbarian - Path of the Berserker (PHB 2014)"
level: 10
enabled: true
notes: "Use your action to frighten one creature you can see within 30 feet, provided it can see or hear you. It makes a Wisdom saving throw against a DC of 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier. On a failure, it is frightened until the end of your next turn. You may use your action on later turns to extend the effect until the end of your following turn. The effect ends if the creature ends its turn more than 60 feet from you or cannot see or hear you. A creature that succeeds on the save cannot be affected by this feature again for 24 hours."
